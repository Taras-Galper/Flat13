Principles

Six rules, every room

Light the walls, not the floor

We judge how bright a room is by its walls. Grazers on the limewash, the clay and the oak fluting make rooms read larger and calmer, and the ceiling stays quiet. The living room has no downlights at all.

Never see the source

Every LED sits in a slot, a curtain pocket or under a plinth. The few downlights sit 35 mm deep, so from the sofa you see a glow and never a bright dot.

One colour of light

2700 K everywhere, with high colour rendering so oak, linen and skin look right. The paper lamps and the night lights warm to 1800 K as they dim, like a flame. Only the office is tunable.

Protect the view

At night a bright room turns the 3 m slider into a mirror. Keep it dim near the glass and give the balcony a little light, and your eye looks through to the bay.

Three heights of light

Floor level for the night path and floating joinery, eye level for lamps, faces and art, ceiling line for the walls. The low layer is what gives a boutique hotel its calm.

Scenes, not switches

Each room has two to four scenes on a small keypad, and the public rooms follow the sunset by themselves. Nobody has to adjust a dimmer.

From late sun to blue hour

The living room faces north, so the sun never enters it directly. Late in the day the sun lights the bay outside instead, and through the west windows it falls into the main bedroom and bedroom 4. The electric light takes over as the daylight goes.

Room by room

In the order of the walk-through

מבואה

Entry

The first thing you see is the glowing bench niche in the oak shoe cabinet, with the cabinet floating on its own line of light. One downlight lights the face of whoever is at the door. A ceiling slot grazes the slatted screen, so its slats throw stripes of shadow across the floor. Behind the slats the paper lantern glows. The east wall is lit all the way from the door to the glass, and it leads you in.

ח.דיור

Living room

No downlights. Two lit walls face each other: the guiding wall behind the sofa, with two pinspots on the sumi canvas, and the clay media wall opposite, grazed so its trowel marks show. The washi pendant is the one lamp you sit under. The olive tree is gone. In its place a 1.60 m paper column stands in the corner by the glass, and at dusk its glow balances the bright sky, so the room does not go flat against the view. The sheers glow from their pocket when drawn. At night the Bay view scene drops all of this to embers.

מטבח

Kitchen and peninsula

This is the one place for bright, practical light. Downlights sit over the front edge of the counter, so you never stand in your own shadow, and a deep line under the oak shelf lights the limestone splash evenly. Two small washi pendants hang 75 cm above the peninsula, so faces at the bar are lit while eyes are not. A toe-kick line and a glow under the overhang make the kitchen read as floating furniture when the task lights are off.

מסדרון · ש.אורחים

Corridor and guest WC

Downlights only at the door thresholds, at 30% in the evening, and low step lights along the south wall for the night. The guest WC is the dark jewel box. A halo glows behind the round mirror and one pinspot lights the stone basin, while the dark plank and taupe clay stay in shadow. It is the most dramatic two square metres in the flat.

ח.עבודה ומשחקים

Office and game room

A direct/indirect pendant over the desk puts 70% of its light on the ceiling. That gives 500 lx on the desk with no glare on the screen. It is tunable, cooler for focused work by day and warm after 18:00. Behind you on a video call the fluted wardrobe is grazed, so the background has depth rather than a black hole. For games, everything drops except a bias light behind the screen, which can go any colour.

  • O1 desk
  • L1 call backdrop
  • B1 RGBW
  • L2 curtain

ממ"ד · ח.שינה

Teen room (safe room)

Safe-room rules allow no recessed fittings, so everything here is surface-mounted or plugs in. A slim surface track with three small spots lights the bed wall, the desk and the wardrobe. Profiles either side of the dressing mirror light her face evenly, as in a make-up studio. A halo behind the linen headboard is warm by default, and she can turn it any colour she likes.

  • T1 track
  • L5 mirror
  • P5 lamp
  • R1 reading
  • L3 RGBW halo

ח.ילדה

Toddler room

Nothing bright to look up into from a floor bed. A flush opal disc over the play rug, not over the bed, dims to 0.1%. A warm line under the top book ledge is the story light. At night an 1800 K amber glow under the toy shelves gives less than 2 lx with no blue in it. All of it is fixed in place and out of reach, with no lamps or cords.

  • K1 disc
  • L4 story
  • L3 night
  • L2 curtain

Main suite

The wardrobe walk is grazed along 3.50 m of oak fluting, with a light line at its foot. In the bedroom the sheer behind the bed glows from its pocket and takes the place of a headboard light. Two articulated reading lights let one of you read without waking the other. The wall you look at from bed gets a graze and a pinspot on the art. Under the bed a 5% line comes on when your foot touches the floor. In the en-suite there is mirror light on the face, a niche line in the shower and a single IP65 downlight.

Evening ערב

Blue hour to 22:00 · keypad: Entry · living

Walls lit, ceiling quiet, three paper glows. Light sits low and warm, like a ryokan after dark.

Any time · keypad: Kitchen

Full task light on the worktop and the peninsula; the living room stays at evening level so the open space still feels calm.

Bay view נוף

After 20:00 · keypad: Living: bottom button

Glass turns into a mirror when the room is brighter than the night. Everything near the slider drops to a glow and the balcony comes up a little, so you see Haifa Bay's lights instead of yourself.

Film סרט

Any time · keypad: Living

Bias light behind the screen, the console floating at 10%, the art barely there. Enough to find your glass.

Bedtime שינה

21:00 onwards · keypad: Each bedroom

Public rooms fade out over ten minutes. Reading lights, paper lamps and the story light, all at 2200 K or warmer.

Night path לילה

A foot on the floor brings up a 5% amber line: under the bed, along the wardrobe walk, at the step lights to the bathroom. Nothing at eye level.

Building it

Notes for the electrician

An 80 mm ceiling void

This assumes a 2.70 m slab. A gypsum ceiling dropped to 2.62 m in the entry, living room, kitchen and corridor hides the slots and the trimless downlights. The bedrooms need only a perimeter slot where there is a grazer or a curtain pocket.

Safe room

Home Front Command rules restrict cutting into the safe room's walls and ceiling. Everything there is surface-mounted or plugged in. Confirm with your contractor before any work.

One system of scenes

Casambi (wireless, good for a renovation) or KNX with DALI-2 drivers if the walls are open. Keypads go at the entry, the living room, the kitchen and each bed. Use flicker-free drivers that dim smoothly down to 0.1%.

Match every LED

Buy all strips from one maker and one colour bin (≤ 2-step MacAdam) so the lines match. Use dot-free COB strip, because the ivory tile has enough sheen to mirror a dotted strip.

Sea air

At 13 floors above Haifa Bay, use IP65–IP67 fittings in marine-grade housings (anodised aluminium or 316 stainless) on the balcony.

  • The olive tree is replaced by the paper column lamp, with more styling: books, throws, ceramics.
