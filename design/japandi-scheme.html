The Bay Apartmentדירת המפרץ · קומה 13, חיפה

A five-room flat on the 13th floor, facing north over Haifa Bay with the sea to the west. The layout stays exactly as drawn. The scheme brings Japanese restraint and Scandinavian warmth to it: one ivory floor, pale oak joinery, limewashed walls, paper light and low furniture, so that the bay stays the main event.

Principles

Five rules for every room

Keep the horizon low

In the living room nothing rises above 0.75 m except the olive tree and the lantern. Seats sit at 0.38 m, so you look over the furniture to the bay.

One floor, edge to edge

Your ivory 60×60 tile everywhere but two rooms, on a single grid with 2 mm joints in a matching grout. Wood stays in the joinery and the rugs bring the warmth.

Storage disappears

About 10 m of floor-to-ceiling fluted oak in the genkan, the wardrobe walk, the bedrooms and the kitchen column. Open surfaces hold almost nothing, except the toddler's toy shelves, which are meant to be reached.

Light through paper

No grid of ceiling downlights. Washi pendants, paper lanterns and hidden light lines, all warm and dimmable.

Texture over colour

An almost monochrome palette that gets its depth from bouclé, limewash, grain and jute. Each room has one sage note and one dark note.

Materials

Twelve materials, nothing else

The two floor samples are cut from your showroom photos. The others are free CC0 Poly Haven textures planned for the 3D build, and smoked oak, washi and sage linen are tinted studies until their textures are sourced.

Lighting

Three layers, all at 2700 K

Paper

A washi pendant over each table, paper lanterns beside the sofa, and paper bedside lamps. In the office the screen wall gets its own dimmable bias light.

Lines

Hidden LED strips in the curtain pocket, at the foot of the wardrobe walk and under the kitchen shelf. They wash the surfaces without showing a source.

Daylight

The north glass gives steady, shadow-soft light all day. At 17:00 the low western sun reaches the kitchen table, bedroom 4 and the main bed.
